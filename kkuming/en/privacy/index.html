Kkuming Privacy Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026

This is an English translation for convenience. If it differs from the Korean version (https://kimjaejung96.github.io/kkuming/privacy/), the Korean version prevails.

Jaejung Kim (the "Operator") values the personal information of users of the Kkuming app (the "App") and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and related laws.

1. Personal information we collect

The App has no sign-up, the Operator runs no server of its own, and the App does not send your name, contact details, photos or anything else to the Operator. However, when you choose "Watch an ad to save", Google AdMob, which is built into the App, processes the information listed in section 2 (such as an advertising identifier) to show the ad, and that information is transferred abroad (to the United States, section 3). The App also sends anonymous usage statistics and error reports to Google Firebase so we can improve it (section 2). You can turn this off in Settings › Send usage stats & errors.

Legal basis: ad information is processed when you tap "Watch an ad to save" yourself (and, where the law requires consent, when you have consented). Usage statistics and error reports are processed under the Operator's legitimate interest in fixing errors and improving the App (Article 15(1)(6) of the Personal Information Protection Act), and you can turn them off at any time. Where consent is required, they are sent only if you turn them on.

  • Photos: Only the photos you pick are copied into the App's own storage for decorating. They stay on your device and are never sent to the Operator.
  • Project files: What you make (projects) is stored only on your device and is deleted together with the App.
  • Payment information: Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The Operator does not receive card or other payment details. The App only stores whether you have an active pass on the device.

2. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts: The first time you use a font that is not bundled with the App, the font file is downloaded from Google's servers (fonts.gstatic.com). Usual connection information such as your device's IP address may reach Google; your photos and projects are not sent.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: In-app purchases. The App only asks the store which passes you currently own.
  • Google AdMob (rewarded ads): Ads are loaded only when a free user saving a project with Plus items taps "Watch an ad to save" themselves. There are no banner or interstitial ads, and Plus users see no ads.
  • Information processed: advertising identifier (iOS IDFA only if you allow tracking; Android advertising ID), device and OS information, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad impressions and clicks, error and performance diagnostics.
  • Purpose: serving and measuring ads, preventing invalid clicks. Photos and projects are not sent.
  • Consent: Where the law requires consent (such as the EEA and the UK), Google's consent screen (UMP) asks about personalized ads before the first ad. On iOS, the App asks whether to allow tracking right before the first ad; ads work the same if you don't allow it.
  • How to change: any time in the App's "Settings › Ad privacy settings" (shown where consent is required), iOS "Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking", or Android "Settings › Google › Ads".
  • Retention, deletion and details: Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics): To learn which features get used and where the App breaks, so we can fix it.
  • Information processed: a random app instance ID created per install, device and OS information, app version, IP address and the country-level location derived from it, usage records (which templates are opened, how many pages are saved, how the paywall was reached and how a purchase ended, ad results), and error reports. No advertising identifier is used, and nothing is used for ads. Photos, text you type and project contents are not sent.
  • How to turn it off: Settings › Send usage stats & errors in the App. Where consent is required (EEA, UK, Switzerland) it starts off and sends only if you turn it on.
  • Retention: usage records up to 14 months, error reports 90 days.
  • Ad count: To count saves made without an ad when none was available (up to 3 a day) and saves made after watching an ad, only today's date and the counts are stored on the device. The daily number of saves made after watching an ad is also sent with the usage statistics.

3. Transfer of personal information abroad

Under Article 28-8 of the Personal Information Protection Act, personal information is transferred abroad as follows.

  • Recipient: Google LLC (privacy inquiries: https://support.google.com/policies/contact/general_privacy_form)
  • Country: United States
  • Items: advertising identifier (iOS IDFA only if you allow tracking; Android advertising ID), device and OS information, IP address, ad impression and click records, error and performance diagnostics
  • When and how: sent over the network when you tap "Watch an ad to save" and an ad loads, and, where consent is required, when the App checks your ad consent status (before the first ad and when you open Settings)
  • Purpose: serving and measuring ads, preventing invalid clicks
  • Retention period: as set by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  • How to refuse, and what happens: If you don't tap to watch an ad, nothing is transferred. You can still use everything else, such as saving work that uses only free items or "Save without these" (removing Plus items; not available for work started from a Plus template); only "Watch an ad to save" is unavailable. If you don't allow tracking on iOS, the IDFA is not transferred.
  • Firebase: same recipient and country as above. The app instance ID, device and OS information, app version, IP address, usage records and error reports are sent over the network while you use the App, and kept for the periods in section 2 to improve it. Turning off Settings › Send usage stats & errors stops the transfer, and the App works the same.
  • When a font that is not bundled is downloaded, connection information such as your IP address may also reach Google's servers in the United States (section 2, Google Fonts).

4. Permissions

  • Photo library access: to load the photos you pick in the photo picker.
  • Add photos: to save finished images to Photos. Existing photos are not read.

5. Retention and deletion

Except for the information processed by Google AdMob and Firebase (sections 2 and 3), all data stays on your device and is deleted from the device when you delete a project in the App or delete the App. If you have turned on device backup (iCloud Backup, Google backup), the App's data may be included in it depending on your device settings; that backup is between you and Apple or Google, and the Operator has no access to it.

6. Children under 14

  • The App has no sign-up and does not ask your age. For children under 14, the app instance ID, advertising identifier and other information processed while using the App (section 2) require the consent of a legal guardian under Article 22-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act.
  • Users under 14 should use the App with a legal guardian. Without the guardian's consent, turn off Settings › Send usage stats & errors and do not tap "Watch an ad to save". With it off, nothing is sent to Firebase, and if you don't tap to watch an ad, no ad information is processed. Free features still work.
  • A legal guardian can ask to access, delete or stop processing the child's information at the email in section 10.

7. Security measures

  • The Operator runs no server that stores user information, and everything sent to the services in section 2 goes over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Access to the Firebase console, where usage statistics and error reports are viewed, is limited to the Operator.
  • Error reports carry only the kind of error, not error messages that could contain text you typed or your work.

8. Automatic collection and how to refuse it

The App does not use web cookies. It uses an app instance ID created for each install (Firebase) and the advertising identifier (AdMob). Turning off Settings › Send usage stats & errors stops the app instance ID from being used, and you can refuse the advertising identifier as described in section 2, "How to change" (turn off tracking on iOS, delete the advertising ID on Android).

9. Your rights

  • You may ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information. The Operator holds nothing that identifies you, such as a name or contact details, and sees only anonymous statistics and error reports in Firebase; for information processed by Google, use your Google account and device settings (section 2, "How to change") or Google's privacy inquiry form.
  • Photos and projects stored on the device are deleted as soon as you delete them in the App or delete the App.
